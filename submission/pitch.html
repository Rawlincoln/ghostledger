GhostLedger 01 / 10

The problem

The money left.
The borehole is a slab.

African budgets already publish PDFs. The gap is not another portal of the same tables. The gap is a person in a ward who can prove the tank was never welded to the plot the paper paid for.

  • Unaccounted billions sit in audit queries for years.
  • Viral totals mix debt, rumour, and dismissed claims.
  • Reporting a missing clinic can be unsafe.
  • Finding out is not the same as knowing who to write.

GhostLedger 06 / 10

Audit tours

Where citizens become project auditors.

Visit the site. Report what you find, or expose what was paid for and never built. Log the ground. Film it. The trail is public and dated. No account.

This is not a dashboard for Nairobi. It is a field book for the person who can still walk to the empty plot.
